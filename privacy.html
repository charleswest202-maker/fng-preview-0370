Privacy policy

What we collect when you contact Funnel and Grow, why we collect it, and how to have it deleted.

Book a free auditCall (240) 630-2035

What we collect

When you book a free audit, we ask for your name, your mobile number and your trade. If you tick the box, we also record that you agreed to texts. If you become a client, we ask for your business details and, for the audit, your last 90 days of calls and jobs.

We also keep a record of the calls, texts and emails you send us. This site does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Why we collect it

  • To call you about your free audit and answer your questions.
  • To text you about your request, only if you ticked the box. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.
  • To prepare your audit and, if you become a client, to run your service.
  • To keep a record of what we agreed with you.

We never sell your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your details. We share them only with the providers we use to run our business, such as our form and phone services, and only so they can do that work for us, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

If you don't become a client, we delete your details within 12 months of our last contact. If you become a client, we keep them while we work together and for up to 3 years after, for our business and tax records.

How to ask us to delete it

Call or text us and ask. We will delete your details within 30 days and tell you when it's done, unless the law requires us to keep a record.

Call or text (240) 630-2035

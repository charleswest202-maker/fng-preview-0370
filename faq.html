Money

What does it cost?

$2,500 setup, then $1,500 a month per company, with every tool included. Your Google ad budget is on top and goes straight to Google. The pricing page

Do I pay you for each lead?

No. Google charges your ad budget only when a customer contacts you from your ad, and that money goes to Google. Our fee is flat.

Is the ad budget inside the $1,500?

No. We set the budget with you during your audit, and you pay it straight to Google.

When does the monthly fee start?

Setup is paid when you sign up. The $1,500 a month starts the day your ads go live.

Do you mark up phone or software costs?

No. Tools are included in the $1,500.

The guarantee

What does "booked jobs cover our fee" mean?

Each month after the ads go live, the booked jobs we brought in, counted at your average job value from the audit, have to add up to at least $1,500. If they don't, the next month's fee is free. How it's counted

What counts as a booked job?

A confirmed appointment on your calendar, from a lead our system brought in, that actually happens. No-shows and cancellations don't count.

Is the setup refunded?

No. A miss makes the next month's fee free. Setup is not refunded, and your Google ad budget stays yours.

What's my side of it?

Keep the agreed ad budget running, answer or return leads within 5 minutes (a call-back counts), and show us which leads turned into booked jobs.

How it works

Who answers my phone?

You do, the way you do today. We don't answer your calls. What we add is a tracked record of each lead on your dashboard, and a real person who calls your past customers in your company's name.

Do you cold call?

No. A real person calls your own past customers who bought in the last 18 months, people who already know you. New customers find you through your Google ads.

Are the leads sold to other companies?

We don't sell leads, and we sign one company per trade in your area. We can't control everything Google does: Google lets a homeowner ask a few businesses for quotes at once. What we control is who we sign.

I already pay for a CRM or an answering service. Do I need yours?

Tell us on the audit call what you already use, and we'll go through what overlaps before you sign. The price is the same either way.

Why not just use Jobber or Housecall Pro?

Keep them. Jobber and Housecall Pro organize the jobs you already have: scheduling, quotes and invoices. We bring you new jobs: the Google ads, the follow-up calls and the booking. Many owners use both.

Where do you work?

Anywhere Google's ads run. One company per trade in your area. Tell us where you work and we'll say on the audit call whether your area is open.

Will you text me?

Only if you tick the box on the form. Otherwise we call.

The agreement

How long is the agreement?

90 days to start, then month to month with 30 days' notice. Nothing renews into another long term.

What happens if I leave?

Your website, Google profile, phone number and customer list are in your name. You keep them if you leave.

What's in the free audit?

Your last 90 days (missed calls, jobs booked, average job value), an after-hours test call, a Google profile and website check, and whether Google's ads that charge only when a customer contacts you are available in your area. You keep the findings. The audit page

Are you new?

Yes. We don't have client results to show you yet, and we won't pretend to. That's why the guarantee exists.

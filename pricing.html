Pricing questions

Is the ad budget inside the $1,500?

No. The $1,500/mo is our fee, with every tool included. Your Google ad budget is on top, paid straight to Google. Before you spend anything, we go through Google's own cost estimate for your area with you during the audit.

Do you mark up phone or software costs?

No. Tools are included in the $1,500. The only cost outside our fee is your Google ad budget.

Is the setup refunded under the guarantee?

No. If we miss the monthly test, the next month's $1,500 fee is free. The setup is not refunded, and your Google ad budget stays yours. How it's counted

When does the monthly fee start?

Setup is paid when you sign up. The $1,500 a month starts the day your ads go live.

How long is the agreement?

90 days to start, then month to month with 30 days' notice. Nothing renews into another long term.

Why not just use Jobber or Housecall Pro?

Keep them. Jobber and Housecall Pro organize the jobs you already have: scheduling, quotes and invoices. We bring you new jobs: the Google ads, the follow-up calls and the booking. Many owners use both.

What happens if I leave?

Your website, Google profile, phone number and customer list are in your name. You keep them if you leave.

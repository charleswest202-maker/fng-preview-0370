Questions roofers ask first

Do I pay you for each lead?

No. Our fee is flat, $2,500 setup + $1,500/mo, with the tools included. Your Google ad budget goes straight to Google, and Google charges it only when a customer contacts you from your ad.

I already pay for a CRM or an answering service. Do I need yours?

Tell us on the audit call what you already use, and we'll go through what overlaps before you sign. The price is the same either way.

Are the leads sold to other roofers?

We don't sell leads, and we sign one roofing company per area. We can't control everything Google does: Google lets a homeowner ask a few businesses for quotes at once. What we control is who we sign, and in your area that's one roofing company.

What does "booked jobs cover our fee" mean?

A booked job is a confirmed appointment on your calendar, from a lead our system brought in, that actually happens. Each month after the ads go live, booked jobs times your average job value have to reach $1,500. If they don't, the next month's fee is free.

How it's counted

Is there a contract?

Yes, a short one: 90 days to start, then month to month. Stop with 30 days' notice. Nothing renews into another long term.

Who calls my past customers?

A real person, in your company's name, and only your own past customers who bought in the last 18 months. Never strangers, never a bought list. You'll hear who it is and what they say on the audit call, before any call goes out.

What happens if I leave?

Your website, Google profile, phone number and customer list are in your name. You keep them if you leave.

Are you new?

Yes. We don't have client results to show you yet, and we won't pretend to. That's why the guarantee exists.

What happens on the free audit?

We look at your last 90 days (missed calls, jobs booked, average job value), make an after-hours test call to your business line, check your Google profile and website, and check whether Google's ads that charge only when a customer contacts you are available in your area.

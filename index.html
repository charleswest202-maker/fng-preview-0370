Straight answers

Do I pay you for each lead?

No. Google charges your ad budget only when a customer contacts you from your ad, and that money goes straight to Google. Our fee is flat: $2,500 setup + $1,500/mo, all-inclusive.

Is the ad budget inside the $1,500?

No. The $1,500/mo is our fee, with every tool included. Your Google ad budget is on top, paid straight to Google, not to us. We set the budget with you during your audit.

What does "booked jobs cover our fee" mean?

Each month after the ads go live, the booked jobs we brought in, counted at your average job value, have to add up to at least $1,500. If they don't, the next month's fee is free. The full terms are on the guarantee page.

How it's counted

Can I cancel?

After the first 90 days, yes: it's month to month, with 30 days' notice.

What happens if I leave?

Your website, Google profile, phone number and customer list are in your name. You keep them if you leave.

Who answers my phone?

You do, the way you do today. We don't answer your calls. What we add is a tracked record of each lead on your dashboard, and a real person who calls your past customers in your company's name.

Are you new?

Yes. We don't have client results to show you yet, and we won't pretend to. That's why the guarantee exists.

How it's counted

Six points. Each one is in your agreement, word for word.

Booked job

A confirmed appointment on the owner's calendar, from a lead our system brought in. It counts only if the appointment actually happens (no-shows and cancellations don't count).

Value

Each booked job counts at the client's average job value, taken from their free audit (last 90 days). No invoices, no self-reported sales.

Test

Monthly, after launch. Booked jobs × average job value must be at least $1,500. The clock starts when the ads go live, not at signup.

If we miss

The next month's $1,500 fee is free. Setup is not refunded. Google ad spend is still the client's.

Client's side

Keep the agreed ad budget running; answer or return leads within 5 minutes (AI answering counts); give us visibility of outcomes.

Exit

After two misses in a row, either side can leave with no notice.

Questions about the guarantee

Is my Google ad budget part of it?

No. Your ad budget is paid straight to Google, not to us, so it isn't refunded under the guarantee. The guarantee is about our monthly fee.

Does a lead count, or only a booked job?

Only a booked job: a confirmed appointment on your calendar, from a lead our system brought in, that actually happens. Leads that never book don't count toward the test.

Who decides my average job value?

Your own numbers from the free audit: your last 90 days. It's written into the agreement before the ads start, so neither side can move it later.

What if I can't answer a lead in 5 minutes?

The guarantee asks you to answer or return leads within 5 minutes. A call-back counts, and so does an answering service of your own. If leads go unanswered, that month's test doesn't apply.

Can I leave if it isn't working?

After two misses in a row, either side can leave with no notice. Otherwise the agreement is 90 days to start, then month to month with 30 days' notice.
